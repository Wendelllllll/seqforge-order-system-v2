04 / PACKAGING

Label and package your samples

SINGLE TUBES

1.5 mL tubes with pop caps

Use 1.5 mL microcentrifuge tubes with pop caps, labeled to match the order form.

For orders of 24 samples or more, use 96-well plates instead of tube strips.

96-WELL PLATES

Normalize and seal

Normalize DNA and primer concentrations according to the applicable preparation requirements. Arrange samples vertically: A1–H1, then A2–H2, and continue by column.

Seal or cap the plate with strip lids to prevent leakage.

For plate submissions, use one primer arrangement:

  1. A single primer for all samples.
  2. A matching primer plate, aligned well-to-well with the DNA plate, when using more than one primer.
  3. Primer already mixed with the DNA in each well.
