Whole plasmid or linear / amplicon?

Choose Whole Plasmid for purified circular constructs. Choose Linear / Amplicon for purified PCR products, amplicon pools or native linear DNA projects.

What files will I receive?

Whole plasmid projects include consensus FASTA, read-length statistics, a pLannotate plot and a feature annotation table. Reference-based variant reporting requires a reference and an agreed scope.

Can PCR products retain native modifications?

PCR does not preserve the original DNA’s native modification pattern. Modification analysis requires suitable PCR-free input and supported analysis methods.

Is local pickup available?

Pickup is available in select areas. Call (858) 492-0518 to confirm coverage, scheduling and packaging.

    BEFORE YOU PREPARE

    Sample requirements

    Sample preparation
    DNA quality & quantity
    Use fluorometric quantification (Qubit or equivalent). For plasmids, a gel can help verify a single construct at the expected size. Prepare purified plasmids in 10 mM Tris or nuclease-free water.
    Purity guidance
    DNA A260/A280 is typically about 1.8, and A260/A230 about 2.0–2.2. Low ratios can indicate contaminants; ratios alone do not guarantee acceptance. NanoDrop guidance ↗
    Labels & plate positions
    Use unique sample IDs matching your containers. One plate per plan, A1–H12. Confirm concentration, volume and container acceptance before dispatch.
    Native modifications
    Modification analysis needs suitable PCR-free input, preparation and supported models. PCR products do not retain the original DNA’s native modification pattern. Nanopore guidance ↗

    Whole Plasmid Sequencing — coverage, applications & files

    Unlock the full potential of plasmid analysis.

    Long reads help characterize complete plasmids and complex vectors, bringing sequence and structural context together. Real-time data acquisition and scalable workflows support projects from individual constructs to larger batches.

    Use whole plasmid sequencing for synthetic construct validation, therapeutic-vector research, microbial plasmid studies and manufacturing research quality control. Consensus analysis can help investigate insertions, deletions and structural differences.

    Delivered with your analysis

    Consensus FASTA, read-length statistics, a pLannotate plasmid plot and an annotated feature table. Reference-based variant reporting is available when a reference is provided and included in the agreed scope.

    Submit pure, double-stranded clonal plasmid DNA. Input quantity, achievable coverage, timing and pricing are confirmed for your construct and batch.

    Linear / Amplicon Sequencing — targets, applications & files

    Keep your target in context.

    Long-read sequencing can span full-length targets and preserve phase across variants. Discuss full-length 16S and ribosomal operons, viral targets, HLA loci, synthetic fragments, large gene regions and CRISPR validation with our team.

    Submit purified PCR products or cleaned amplicon pools, free of primers and dimers. The supplied preparation guidance is 50–100 ng total, depending on pool complexity; confirm your project’s input requirement. Include expected sizes and primer sequences, or request design support.

    Delivered with your analysis

    Consensus FASTA per barcode, read-length and coverage plots, and consensus quality statistics. Reference alignment, variant analysis and phylogenetic placement are optional. Modification reports require an appropriate native, PCR-free workflow and supported targets.

    Library preparation, multiplexing, sequencing depth and analysis are tailored to your project. Read length, turnaround and accuracy depend on input quality and agreed scope.

    Expert support, flexible projects

    SeqForge supports academic, biotechnology and pharmaceutical research with application planning and analysis support. Discuss construct comparison, mutation checks, project scale and data delivery with our team.
