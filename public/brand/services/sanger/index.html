Common questions

Can you work with difficult templates?

Discuss our difficult-template workflow for GC-rich regions, repetitive sequences, secondary structures and long homopolymers.

Do I need to send my own primer?

You can supply your own primer or select an appropriate universal primer. Confirm availability and suitability before preparing samples.

Is PCR cleanup available?

Enzymatic PCR cleanup removes unincorporated primers and dNTPs before sequencing. Ask our team whether your product needs cleanup.

Is local pickup available?

Pickup is available in select areas. Call (858) 492-0518 to confirm coverage, scheduling and packaging.
