What can I use Fragment Analysis for?

RFLP, ITDs and microsatellites are among the applications that can be studied using capillary electrophoresis. Discuss your assay and interpretation needs with our team.

What are the submission requirements?

Requirements depend on the application. Confirm labels, concentration, volume and expected fragment sizes before sending samples.

How quickly can I get results?

Turnaround can start from next day for applicable services. Confirm timing and cutoffs for your assay and location.

Is local pickup available?

Pickup is available in select areas. Call (858) 492-0518 to confirm coverage, scheduling and packaging.
